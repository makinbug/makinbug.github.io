---
title: "Privacy"
description: "This site uses no cookies and no tracking. How GitHub and eBay handle visitor and order data."
---

This site uses no cookies and no tracking. It has no analytics, no adverts and no contact form.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security. We do not see these logs. GitHub explains how it uses them in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you email us, we use your address and what you send only to reply and to do the work.

Products are sold on eBay. eBay handles the order, the payment and the postage under its own privacy policy.
