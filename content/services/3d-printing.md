title: "3D printing"
description: "3D printing to order on a high precision delta printer, in one or two colours, with soluble supports."
---

We print to order on a high precision delta printer. It has two extruders, so it can print in two colours, or with soluble supports.

Parts print to 0.1 mm accuracy, part permitting.

We print in PLA and PET, and use PVA for soluble supports. We design parts to print without supports where we can, to keep waste low.

Send us your own design, or we can [design the part for you](/services/cad-design/).

**How to order:** email [info@makinbug.uk](mailto:info@makinbug.uk) with your file or a description of the part.
