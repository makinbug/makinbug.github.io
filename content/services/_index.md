Makin'Bug does three things. Each is made to order.

- [CAD design](/services/cad-design/): 3D models and 2D drawings in Fusion 360.
- [3D printing](/services/3d-printing/): parts printed on a high precision delta printer.
- [Laser engraving and cutting](/services/laser/): engraved and cut parts.

To order, email [info@makinbug.uk](mailto:info@makinbug.uk) with what you need.
