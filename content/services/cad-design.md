We design in Fusion 360. We make full 3D solid CAD models or 2D detail drawings.

We can start a design from scratch, or change a model you already have. No job is too small.

Typical jobs are adapters, racks and mounts, and parts made to fit something you already own.

**How to order:** email [info@makinbug.uk](mailto:info@makinbug.uk) with what you need. A sketch, a photo or a measurement helps.
