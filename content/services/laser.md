We engrave and cut with a laser. Engrave a design, a name or a logo, or cut shapes to size.

Send us your design file. If you do not have one, we can [draw it for you in CAD](/services/cad-design/).

Tell us the material and the size you want, and we will say what we can do.

**How to order:** email [info@makinbug.uk](mailto:info@makinbug.uk) with your design, the material and the size.
