Makin'Bug designs and makes custom parts in Telford: CAD design, 3D printing, and laser engraving and cutting.

## [CAD design](/services/cad-design/)

Full 3D models and 2D drawings in Fusion 360. No job is too small.

## [3D printing](/services/3d-printing/)

Printed to order on a high precision delta printer, in one or two colours.

## [Laser engraving and cutting](/services/laser/)

Engraved and cut parts, from your design or one we draw for you.
